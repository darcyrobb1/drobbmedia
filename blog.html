Frequently asked questions

Sample answers for common DRobbMedia booking, delivery, and shoot-prep questions.

How far ahead should I book a shoot?

For events and game-day coverage, booking two to four weeks ahead is ideal. Short-notice bookings can still work when the date is available.

What types of photography does DRobbMedia cover?

DRobbMedia covers sport, events, commercial imagery, team content, portraits, and highlight-ready social media assets.

How quickly are photos delivered?

Delivery depends on the size of the shoot. A small set of priority images can usually be delivered first, with the full edited gallery following after selection and editing.

Can I request specific shots before the day?

Yes. A short shot list is useful for sponsors, key people, team photos, venue details, or moments that must be captured.

Do you shoot in low light or night games?

Yes. Night games and indoor events are possible, but the final look depends on the venue lighting and access around the action.

Can the photos be used on social media?

Edited images can be prepared for web and social use. Usage terms should be confirmed when booking, especially for commercial campaigns or paid advertising.

Do you offer commercial photography for brands?

Yes. Commercial shoots can be built around staff portraits, product visuals, campaign imagery, location content, or social media content libraries.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Include the date, location, shoot type, approximate coverage time, delivery needs, and any key moments or people that need priority coverage.
